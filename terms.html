Terms of Service

These terms apply when you use the Urban Drive Motors website ("the website"). By using the website, creating an account, sending us a message or submitting a form, you agree to them. If you do not agree, please do not use the website.

1. Who we are

Urban Drive Motors ("we", "us") sells lorries, cars and heavy machinery in Kenya. You can reach us on +254 740 757 592 or at bluelink_solutions@outlook.com.

2. Using the website

  • You must be 18 or older to create an account or make an enquiry.
  • Give us true and complete information, and keep your password private. You are responsible for what happens under your account.
  • Do not misuse the website. That includes trying to break into it, copying it in bulk by automated means, sending spam, or posting anything unlawful, false or abusive.
  • We may suspend or close an account that breaks these terms or puts the website or other users at risk.

3. Vehicle listings and prices

  • We work to keep listings accurate, but descriptions, photos, mileage, specifications and availability are given in good faith and can change or contain mistakes. Please confirm the details with us before you decide.
  • Prices are shown in Kenya shillings (KES). If a price is wrong, we may correct it and will tell you before you commit.
  • A vehicle is not reserved or sold to you until we have agreed it with you directly and any agreed deposit or payment has been received and confirmed by us.
  • We encourage you to view and inspect any vehicle before buying, and to ask to see the logbook and ownership papers.

4. Viewings, test drives and bookings

Viewings and test drives are by appointment. We may ask for identification and a valid driving licence before a test drive, and we may cancel or reschedule an appointment if needed. We will tell you if we do.

5. Selling or trading in your vehicle

  • Sending a vehicle through the sell form is an invitation to talk. It is not an offer to buy, and we are not obliged to buy, list or trade in any vehicle.
  • You confirm that you own the vehicle or are allowed to sell it, and that the details and photos you send are true and are yours to share.
  • You allow us to use the photos and details you send to assess your vehicle and, if we agree to list it, to show it on the website.
  • We may ask you for ownership documents. Keep them private and share them only with us through the way we ask.

6. Messages and enquiries

When you send an enquiry or message, you agree that we may reply by phone, WhatsApp, email or on the website, using the details you gave us.

7. Careers and job applications

  • Applying for a role, or sending us your CV, does not create a job or a promise of one. We decide who to interview and hire.
  • Everything in your application must be true. We may check references and qualifications with your agreement.
  • How we handle your CV and details is explained in the Privacy Policy below.

The website design, text, logos and other material belong to Urban Drive Motors or are used with permission. Please do not copy or reuse them without our written permission. Photography credits are shown on the website footer.

Privacy Policy

We respect your privacy and handle personal information in line with the Data Protection Act, 2019 of Kenya. This section explains what we collect, why, who sees it and what your choices are.

1. What we collect

  • Account details: your email address, phone number, name if you give it, and how you heard about us. Your password is stored securely by our login system and we cannot read it.
  • Enquiries and messages: what you write to us, the vehicle you ask about, and when.
  • Bookings: the vehicle, date and time you choose for a viewing or test drive.
  • Selling a vehicle: your contact details, the vehicle details, photos, the price you want, and the ownership or document status you tell us.
  • Job applications: your name, email, phone number, location, experience, links and message, and the CV you upload.
  • Which vehicles you open: when you are logged in, the website records which vehicle pages you view, so we can follow up on what interests you.
  • Browser storage: small items saved in your browser to keep you logged in and remember light or dark mode.

2. Why we use it

  • To answer your enquiries and messages, and to arrange viewings and test drives.
  • To run your account and keep it secure.
  • To review vehicles you want to sell or trade in.
  • To consider job applications and contact applicants.
  • To understand which vehicles are popular and improve the website.
  • To meet legal duties and protect ourselves and others from fraud or misuse.

We use your information because you ask us to (for example when you make an enquiry), because you agree to it, because it is needed to deal with you, because we have a genuine business reason that does not override your rights, or because the law requires it.

3. Who can see it

  • Our staff who need it for their work. Staff only see the parts of the admin area they are given access to.
  • Our database and hosting provider, which stores the information for us under our instructions.
  • Job applications and CVs are kept private and are only opened by people involved in hiring.
  • Authorities or other parties where the law requires or allows us to share it.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Where it is kept and for how long

Our database is hosted on servers in the European Union (Frankfurt). Where information is stored or handled outside Kenya, we take steps to protect it as the law requires. We keep information only as long as we need it for the reasons above or the law requires, and then delete or anonymise it. You can ask us to delete your information sooner.

5. Keeping it safe

We use access controls, secure connections and private storage for sensitive files such as CVs. No system is completely secure, so please keep your password private and tell us straight away if you think your account has been misused.

6. Your rights

Under the Data Protection Act, 2019 you can:

  • ask what personal information we hold about you and get a copy;
  • ask us to correct information that is wrong or incomplete;
  • ask us to delete information we no longer need;
  • object to us using your information for a particular purpose;
  • withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting what we did before; and
  • complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner if you think we have not handled your information properly.

To use any of these rights, contact us using the details below. We may need to confirm who you are first.

7. Children

The website is not meant for people under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

We may update this policy and will change the date at the top when we do.
